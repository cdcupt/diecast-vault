Support

Diecast Vault turns your 1:64 scale diecast collection into a living 3D display cabinet on your phone. Here is how to get the most out of it — and how to reach us.

Getting started

Open the Cabinet tab to see your real-time 3D display cabinet. Drag to rotate it, and switch between cabinet styles from the style picker. Use the Catalog tab to browse models and open any car's detail page, where you can choose left- or right-hand drive.

Scanning your own cars

On a supported Pro iPhone, you can scan your own diecast cars into 3D models using the camera. All scanning is processed on your device — nothing is uploaded. If scanning is unavailable on your device, you'll still enjoy the full cabinet, catalog, and library.

Frequently asked questions

Is Diecast Vault free?
Yes. The app is free to download and use.
Does it collect my data?
No. Diecast Vault collects no personal data and requires no account. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Where is my collection stored?
On your device, and in your own private iCloud if enabled, so it follows you across your Apple devices.
Can I use it in Chinese?
Yes. Diecast Vault is fully bilingual in English and 简体中文, and you can switch languages live in the app.
Why can't I scan a car?
On-device 3D scanning needs a recent Pro iPhone with the required camera and sensors. The rest of the app works on all supported devices.
