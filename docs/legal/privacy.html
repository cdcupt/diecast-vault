Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

Diecast Vault is built to keep your collection yours. The app collects no personal data, requires no account, and keeps your collection on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Diecast Vault does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Where your collection is stored

Your collection and preferences are stored locally on your device. Nothing leaves your phone: there is no server, no account, and no upload.

Third parties

Diecast Vault contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking. We do not share data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Children

Diecast Vault does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
