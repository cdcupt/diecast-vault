4 Spikes (go/no-go)

Spike-1 — 3D cabinet GO (sim)

Real-time RealityKit cabinet renders correctly; ~60 fps / 40 MB / thermal nominal in the simulator at 24 niches. Renderer sits behind a swappable seam (a NO-GO is a swap, not a rewrite). Device-bound caveat: sim ≠ device GPU, and real 25 MB-USDZ LOD cost is not yet exercised — re-confirm on a physical device.

Spike-0 — scan quality PENDING — needs PM device

Whether a Pro iPhone can turn a real glossy/cased Mini GT into a good model is the biggest product risk and cannot be run by an agent or the simulator. The capture flow is built, Pro-gated, and camera-permission-ready; the PM runs the go/no-go on a physical Pro iPhone with real cars at Gate 3.

7 Scope & what's deferred

This is a v1 simulator build on DEMO content. Real 3D models come from user scans (Spike-0) + the community library; car catalog, the Real-Car enrichment (Wikidata/Wikipedia/Commons + AI), and community upload are v1.1 backend (own diecast-postgres + Cloudflare R2, BillMind-style deploy). The DMCA §512(c) designated agent is a launch-blocker for the community feature, not for the local v1.
