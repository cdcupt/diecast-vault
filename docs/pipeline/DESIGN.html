1 Design principles & the chosen direction

Vitrine — the lit display cabinet (light-as-state)

The PM's physical collection is a wall-mounted cabinet of lit cells under a glowing lightbar. We translate that 1:1 rather than invent a metaphor. The one idea that earns the direction: light is state. A lit niche is an owned/present scan; a dark-matte recess is a known release with no scan yet. Capability (Pro vs non-Pro), ownership, and empty slots are all communicated by luminance — never by banners or greyed-out buttons. That is the PRD's pick-first / scan-as-contribution ethos made visual.

The ownable metaphor, explained

Recess, not card

Cells are carved into the warm bone paper (inverse of an elevated Material card), sitting in a faint shadow well. Depth comes from a value-gap — pure-white faces lifting off --paper — plus a single soft contact shadow under each car.

Light spills downward

A lit niche pools --tungsten-glow at its base — the display-light the car catches. The glow is pre-baked into the cell background so long scrolls never pay a per-cell blur cost.

Travel to the dark

Dark is a place you go, not a theme. You lift a car out of its niche onto the 3D stage; the warm chrome cross-dims. Dismiss re-cabinets it. The rest of the app is always warm light.

Anti-template proof — the direction demonstrates (well past the required four)

  • Scale-contrast hierarchy: serif car names + a big lightbar count tower over mono metadata.
  • Intentional rhythm: an enforced bento — one wide feature niche every ~5–6 cells, grouped by shelf-edge hairlines — not uniform padding.
  • Depth / layering: recessed niches, value-gap, contact shadows, the lift-from-case zoom.
  • Typography with character: a real three-voice pairing (serif / SF Pro / mono) each with a job.
  • Semantic color: tungsten=lit/action, steel=system/community, teal/amber=drive — color always means something.
  • Designed states: drive decals snap with rotateY + haptic; accent presses to --tungsten-deep; a tungsten selection ring.
  • Editorial composition: the alternate ruled Index list, mono catalog numbers flush-left.
  • Atmosphere: restrained warm light spill; the dark stage's tungsten horizon rim.

The serif is the emotional anchor for the P0 Picker — it makes each car read as a catalogued objet. 2 Information architecture / screen-map

A standard native four-tab shell (Cabinet · Catalog · Library · Settings), preceded by a one-time capability gate. Modal sheets (drive select, share, scan, bond) and the full-screen 3D viewer overlay the tabs. Nothing fights iOS conventions — the cabinetry lives in surface and light, not in custom navigation.

3 The three key flows

(a) PICK — works on any iPhone

(b) SCAN + BOND [Pro] — the contribution path, honest about Spike-0 quality

(c) SHARE / opt-in upload — per-model consent, attribution, one canonical per (number, drive)

Cross-cutting states (authored, not generic)

empty

No cars yet — an authored cabinet of dark matte niches under the lightbar, each inviting a first pick. The lightbar reads "0 LIT · pick your first car." Not a blank screen.

error

Failed fetch / reconstruct — inline retry on the affected surface (not a blocking modal); the cabinet around it stays usable.

offline

Offline — the cached shelf works fully; the Library tab dims with a steel "reconnect to browse community scans" note. Picks queue is not promised in v1.

not-supported

Scan affordances on non-Pro — rendered as steel-outlined un-lit states framed as contribution, never dead-greyed buttons or modal warnings.

7 Motion principles & accessibility

Motion principles

  • Lift-from-the-case is the signature and the sole justification for going dark: a matched-geometry shared-element zoom — niche glow swells, poster scales up, warm chrome cross-dims to --stage-0, car settles on a spotlit turntable. Dismiss reverses, re-seating the car. The model is never teleported.
  • Light-on bloom on pick: a matte recess fills with --tungsten-glow as the poster fades up — light arriving, not a card appearing.
  • Telemetry odometer-roll on the lightbar counters on sync/pick; one warmer pulse on pull-to-refresh / new pick.
  • Designed touch states: drive decals snap with rotateY + haptic; accent presses to --tungsten-deep; a tungsten selection ring; 3D idle auto-orbit stops on first touch and decelerates with a settle ease.
  • Compositor-friendly only: animate transform, opacity, clip-path, and (sparingly, pre-baked) glow — never width/height/top/left/margin/font-size. Per-niche glow is baked into the cell background; live blur is capped to visible cells.

Accessibility (concrete, per WCAG)

  • Dynamic Type: all chrome uses SF Pro and scales fully. The serif carries a hard minimum size and falls to SF Pro Display at accessibility-XL sizes, so names never go fragile or low-contrast.
  • Color contrast: --ink on --paper ≈ 14:1 and on --cell-surface ≈ 16:1 (far past WCAG AA 4.5:1). Drive is never color-only — the L/R glyph carries it for color-vision deficiency (1.4.1 Use of Color). Tungsten/steel are used for fills with white text checked for ≥4.5:1, or paired with a glyph/label.
  • VoiceOver: a lit niche reads "Toyota GR Supra, MGT00748, left-hand drive, community scan, owned — opens 3D viewer"; an empty niche reads "MGT00219, no community scan yet, double-tap to pick or scan." Light-as-state is mirrored in the accessibility label, never conveyed by color alone.
  • Reduced Motion (first-class path): every signature transition degrades to a clean crossfade/opacity change; auto-orbit, lightbar pulse, and odometer roll are disabled; nothing essential is conveyed by motion alone (2.3.3).
  • Targets & focus: 44pt minimum tap targets, correct safe areas, a visible tungsten focus ring, and full keyboard/switch-control traversal of all controls (2.4.7, 2.5.5).
  • Capability without dead ends: non-Pro states are informational steel notes with the same VoiceOver dignity as actions — no "disabled, dimmed" announcements that read as failure.

8 Open design questions

Empty-niche affordance for non-Pro users: should a matte recess on the Cabinet be tappable to jump straight to Catalog-pick, or stay a passive "known release" marker until reached via Catalog? Lean: tappable → Release detail, since the steel note already frames the next action.
Feature-niche selection: which release earns the wide niche per ~5–6 cells — most-recent pick, a "shelf hero" the user pins, or editorial rotation? Lean: user-pinned, falling back to most-recent, to avoid the fuzzy "why is this big" read that hurt Direction A.
Index vs Cabinet default on first launch with a populated shelf — does a collector who thinks in catalog order want Index remembered as their default? Lean: remember last-used mode per user.
Lightbar count semantics when the library is browsed (not just the personal shelf): does "LIT" ever count library scans, or strictly owned copies? Lean: strictly owned, to keep the Cabinet personal; library has its own count.

None block build. Each carries a default lean; the PM can correct at Gate 1.
