FLOW B Home — The Cabinet

The signature screen, rebuilt as a realistic lit display case: a powder-coated carcass with mitred corners and slim mullions frames a grid of glass niches, each car sitting forward of a graded backboard on a lit shelf — contact shadow, faint floor reflection, one diagonal glass specular, and a tungsten glowbar valance spilling warmth into the case. Real depth via CSS perspective + a press/hover parallax tilt (reduced-motion-safe; it still reads 3D from the static shadow/glass/light layers alone). The case is themeable across 4 shipped styles — the same shelves, dressed differently. Below: the default Lightbar White, then the same cabinet in Walnut Case and Museum Vitrine, plus the Choose your cabinet picker. Tap any lit niche to lift the car onto the dark 3D stage; tap the mono STYLE chip on the lightbar to change vibe in-context.

History

Unveiled in 2022, the 992-generation 911 GT3 RS is the most aggressively aerodynamic road-going 911 Porsche had built, borrowing its swan-neck rear wing and active DRS flaps from the marque’s endurance racing programme.

A naturally-aspirated 4.0-litre flat-six revs to 9,000 rpm and feeds the rear wheels alone through a seven-speed PDK gearbox — no turbos, no hybrid assist, in deliberate contrast to its rivals.

The optional Weissach package trades steel and aluminium for carbon-fibre and magnesium, shedding weight and adding roll-cage bracing for owners who intend to take the car to a circuit.
