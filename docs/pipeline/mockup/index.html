How to use: every screen is rendered in a live iPhone frame, grouped by flow so you can scan them all on one page. Core screens are wired for click-through — tapping a lit niche on Home lifts the car onto the dark 3D stage, “View in AR” opens the AR preview, the tab bar and back chevrons move between screens, and the drive decals / segmented controls toggle in place. Look for the amber tappable affordances.
